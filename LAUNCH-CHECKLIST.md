# Shine Squad website: launch checklist for the owner

Please confirm or supply each item. The site is built and working; these items turn it from "ready" into "live".

## Needed before going live

- [ ] **Where should bookings go?** An email address to receive consultation requests (and/or permission to set up a Google Sheet).
- [ ] **Domain name**, e.g. `shinesquad.in` (buy it, or tell us which one you own).
- [ ] **Google Place ID / Maps API key.** We can set these up with you (free tier; about 10 minutes).
- [ ] **Instagram profile link**, so it can be added to the footer and search data.

## Please confirm these facts (they're on the site now)

- [ ] "A unit of **Car Jewel**, The Art of Car Decor, **since 2002**" (from the Car Jewel branding). OK to show?
- [ ] Hours: **Mon–Sat 10 AM – 10 PM, Sunday closed** (from Google).
- [ ] Brands named on the site: **JBL, JL Audio, Pioneer, Moco, Turtle Wax, Top Gear** (seen on your shelves and in your videos). OK to name them?
- [ ] "Our Car Jewel team was the first in AP & Telangana to install the **Pioneer DMH-AP6650BT**" (from your YouTube video title).
- [ ] Success message after booking: "We'll call you during working hours to fix a time." Is that the right promise? Should we add a time, e.g. "within 2 hours"?
- [ ] Privacy: booking details kept for up to 12 months. OK?

## Nice to have (makes the site even better)

- [ ] **Logo as a vector file** (SVG, AI or PDF) from your signboard maker. The site currently sets the name in matching type.
- [ ] **Prices or "starting from" prices** and typical times for coating, PPF and spa packages.
- [ ] **3–4 before/after photo pairs**, taken from the same angle (paint correction, headlight restoration, interior).
- [ ] **A short hero video** (8–12 s, landscape, 4K): foam rinse, polisher on black paint, water beading on a coated bonnet, the sign lighting up at dusk.
- [ ] **Team photo** and a photo of the sample wall (leatherette swatches, film shades).

## After launch

- [ ] Add the website link to the Google Business Profile, Instagram bio and YouTube channel.
- [ ] Ask happy customers for Google reviews. The site's **Write a review** button takes them straight there.
